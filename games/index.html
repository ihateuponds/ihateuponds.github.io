🎮 FAVORITE GAMES SHRINE

The all-time titles that defined my childhood, fueled my rhythm game obsession, shaped my love for pixel art, and ate thousands of late-night hours.

PlayStation 2 ⚔️
Kingdom Hearts II
Action RPG

Absolute peak PS2 combat, Roxas prologue in Twilight Town, Drive Forms, Reaction Commands, and Yoko Shimomura's soundtrack. No game has ever matched its weight, movement speed, and boss design.

Nintendo DS 🐾
Pokémon SoulSilver
RPG / Adventure

The undisputed pinnacle of 2D Pokémon. Walking Pokémon sprite behind you, two entire regions (Johto + Kanto), the Pokéwalker accessory, and peak Nintendo DS pixel art and chiptune soundfont.

PC / Multi 🌱
Stardew Valley
Farming / Life Sim

The ultimate comfort game. Endless modding, soundtrack on loop, Skull Cavern stress, automated ancient fruit wineries, and the coziest rainy day atmosphere in video games.

Arcade / PC 💃
DDR / StepMania
Rhythm Game

Rhythm game perfection. Playing custom simfiles on StepMania with OutFox engine, custom speed mods, and burning calories at 200+ BPM with hyperpop and EDM tracks.

PC 🚀
Starbound
Space Sandbox

Exploring infinite procedurally generated planets, building space stations, playing as Novakids, and listening to Curtis Schweitzer's incredible ambient synth-orchestral OST.

GameCube 📺
Pokémon Channel
Virtual Pet / Nostalgia

The most delightfully unhinged nostalgic GameCube gem. Watching anime on a retro CRT TV with Pikachu, collecting Nice Cards, watching Pichu Bros episodes, and Smeargle painting.

DC / GCN 🦔
Sonic Adventure 2
3D Platformer / Chao

City Escape, Live and Learn, speedrunning Hero and Dark story stages, and spending hundreds of hours raising immortal Chaos with animals in the Chao Garden.

PlayStation 3 🧵
LittleBigPlanet 2
Creative Sandbox

The creative peak of the PS3 era. Logic microchips, Sackboy customization, grappling hooks, and the golden age of community-made levels and multiplayer lobbies.
