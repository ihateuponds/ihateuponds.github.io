★ HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN MP3 MUSIC TO THE RETRO CD PLAYER ★
========================================================================

1. Put your favorite .mp3 files in this `music/` folder:
   - `bmth-shadow-moses.mp3`
   - `guardin-track.mp3`
   - `femtanyl-diner-ghost.mp3`
   - `brakence-5g.mp3`
   - `suicide-silence-yolo.mp3`
   - `cr1tter-teeth.mp3`

   (Or name them whatever you like and update the track filename in `script.js`!)

2. In `script.js` (around line 95), you can edit the `tracks` playlist list:
   - Change song titles, artist names, genre tags, and the `src` path to point to your files!
   - You can also link directly to online MP3 URLs (e.g., Catbox, Internet Archive, Discord CDN, etc.).

3. When uploading to Neocities:
   - Upload this entire `music/` folder containing your MP3s along with `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`.
========================================================================
