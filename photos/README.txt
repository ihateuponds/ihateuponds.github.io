★ HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOS TO THE POLAROID GALLERY ★
========================================================================

1. Drop your pictures (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP) into this `photos/` folder:
   - `photo1.jpg`
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - `photo4.jpg`

2. In `index.html` (or in Secret Creator Mode `Ctrl+Shift+E`), you can edit the image paths, captions, and dates:
   - Example: `<img src="./photos/photo1.jpg" alt="Ponds" />`
   - Caption: `📍 SC // 2026`

3. When uploading to Neocities:
   - Upload this entire `photos/` folder along with `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`.
========================================================================
