001. What is the story behind your nickname "ihateuponds"?
Friends started calling me Ponds years ago and it completely stuck. The handle "ihateuponds" was born out of self-deprecating irony during late-night gaming sessions and became my permanent cyber alias.
002. Why did you decide to build a Neocities website?
I got sick of modern social media: endless algorithmic feeds, tracking pixels, rage-bait, and corporate walled gardens. Building an authentic personal website lets me own my space, express my love for retro tech and music, and connect with people who actually care about the indie web.
003. What first got you into cybersecurity?
Tinkering with hardware and modding consoles as a kid. Once you realize how software and network protocols actually work underneath the slick user interfaces, securing and auditing systems becomes an addictive puzzle.
004. Why are Ghost-type Pokémon your absolute favorites?
They have the most creative, eerie, and stylish lore in the franchise. Pokémon like Gengar, Mimikyu, and Gholdengo possess incredible utility, immunity to normal attacks, and an unmistakable chaotic energy.
005. What is your favorite Pokémon game of all time?
Pokémon SoulSilver on Nintendo DS. Two entire regions (Johto + Kanto), physical/special split, Pokémon following you, and the peak of 2D pixel art. Second place goes to Pokémon Emerald.
006. Why choose Arch Linux as your daily driver?
Complete control over every single package and process. Combined with Hyprland and Wayland, it offers instantaneous window tiling, bleeding-edge kernels, and no bloatware telemetry running in the background.
007. What drew you into hyperpop and fast electronic music?
The sheer auditory adrenaline. When artists push tempos past 200 BPM, distort the 808s, and layer pitched vocals over chiptune synths, it creates an intense dopamine rush that regular pop simply cannot match.
008. Why collect physical Pokémon TCG cards?
In an era where everything is a digital rental, holding real cards with textured holographic foil and special illustration artwork hits completely different. Finding cards like the Hidden Fates Jessie & James or Tag Team grails is pure nostalgia.
009. What is your obsession with LSD Dream Emulator?
Osamu Sato 1998 PS1 creation is the pinnacle of interactive liminal surrealism. No objectives, no combat, no linear path: just wandering through subconscious environments where textures swap and music modulates on every step.
